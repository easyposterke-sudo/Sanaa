# Reference title

- **ID:** stable identifier
- **Category / subcategory:** parent / child
- **Tags:** content features and visual families
- **Source:** user-supplied filename or documented source
- **Editable source:** path, or unavailable
- **Status:** annotation status and user feedback
- **Asset reuse:** recorded permission or reference-only status

## Suitable brief and design character

What this approach suits; mood and intended audience.

## Composition and hierarchy

Regions, reading order, anchors, grouping, proportions, and whitespace.

## Typography, colour, and imagery

Observed treatments and relationships. Identify uncertain techniques; do not invent exact fonts, colours, or source-layer construction.

## Functional decoration

Describe each major decorative element and its purpose.

## Content meaning

Required and optional content; distinguish example wording from reusable structure.

## Adaptation rules

Explain how to handle missing images, longer wording, different counts, dates, themes, and palettes.

## Preserve and vary

Relationships to preserve, choices to vary, and situations where another recipe fits better.

## Quality checks

Checks on the rendered result, including small-size readability and factual accuracy.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Separate visible facts, user-stated intent, and inferred implementation methods.
